Romance 
Pride and Prejudice - 28 January 1813
Pride and Prejudice is a novel by Jane Austen that satirizes issues of marriage and social class. It follows the relationship between the quick-to-judge Elizabeth Bennet and the haughty Mr. Darcy as both learn to mend their errors in judgment and look beyond markers of social status.

Outlander - 1991 - 2023
The Outlander series by Diana Gabaldon focuses on 20th-century British nurse Claire Randall, who time travels to 18th-century Scotland and finds adventure and romance with the dashing Highland warrior Jamie Fraser.

Jane Eyre - 19 October 1847
A coming of age story about a young girl as she travels from an abusive and lonely childhood to a young woman working as governess for the ward of the brooding Mr. Rochester. Through the ups and downs of life, Jane finds a way to create light and happiness even in the darkest times. The author is Charlotte Brontoe.

Gone with the wind - 1936
Gone with the Wind is a 1936 novel by Margaret Mitchell. It tells the story of a family woman in the American South (and in the plantation of Tara) during the Civil War. She falls in love with a city gentleman. It is one of the most famous books of its time, and was made into a movie of the same name.

Sense and Sensibility - 30 October 1811
The novel follows the three Dashwood sisters and their widowed mother as they are forced to leave the family estate at Norland Park and move to Barton Cottage, a modest home on the property of distant relative Sir John Middleton. There Elinor and Marianne experience love, romance, and heartbreak.
